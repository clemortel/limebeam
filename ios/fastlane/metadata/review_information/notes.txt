LimeBeam is a generic IPTV player. It does NOT provide, host or bundle any
channels, movies or media. Users connect their own Xtream Codes account or
M3U/M3U8 playlist.

NO ACCOUNT IS REQUIRED to review the app. On the welcome screen, tap
"Essayer la demo" / "Try the demo" to load a small set of free, public test
streams (official Apple/Mux HLS samples, Tears of Steel, etc.) and exercise
every feature: live, VOD, playback, floating window, settings, parental lock.

The "Soutenir le developpeur" (Tip Jar) screen uses standard consumable
in-app purchases and unlocks NO content or feature: it is purely an optional
donation.
