LimeBeam is a modern, fast and elegant IPTV player for watching your own streams.

LimeBeam provides no content. The app ships no channels, movies or series: you connect your own IPTV subscription through an Xtream Codes account or an M3U / M3U8 playlist.

WHAT YOU GET
- Live TV, movies and series, organized automatically from your source
- A clear, readable TV guide (EPG)
- Resume playback: pick up exactly where you left off
- Floating window: keep watching while you browse the app
- Fast search and favorites
- Parental control with a PIN code
- Light and dark themes, adjustable font size
- Smooth player with gestures (brightness, volume, seek)

PRIVACY FIRST
- 100% local: your credentials and playlists stay on your device
- No ads, no trackers, no LimeBeam account required

SUPPORT DEVELOPMENT
LimeBeam is free. If you enjoy it, an optional Tip Jar lets you support the developer via in-app purchases: it unlocks no content or feature.

Try the built-in demo from the welcome screen, with no credentials at all.
